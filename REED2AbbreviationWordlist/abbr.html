Abbreviations from REED Collections

A searchable index of scribal abbreviations drawn from the Records of Early English Drama corpus

This is a dictionary wordlist generated from Records of Early English Drama (REED) data as TEI XML as a main format and then JSON for this dataTable generated from that. The source for this is available at: https://github.com/jamescummings/REED/tree/master/REED2AbbreviationWordlist and is licensed CC-BY by @jamescummings.bsky.social.

There are 11735 distinct forms from 51628 abbreviated word instances across 5 languages found in 10 collections. These collections are:

  • Bear Gardens/Hope Playhouse (bghop)
  • Berkshire (berks)
  • Cambridgeshire (cambs)
  • Hampshire (hamps)
  • Newington Butts Playhouse (nwbts)
  • Rose Playhouse (rosep)
  • Staffordshire (staff)
  • Suffolk (suffk)
  • York (yorkc)
  • Yorkshire North Riding (yksnr)

You can sort and filter by each column or generally overall.
If you want to filter the expansion column for an expanded word containing certain non-contiguous letters, separate them by a space, e.g. 'a p i', and it should find them in any order.
